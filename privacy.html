Privacy policy

My Makeup Collection · last updated 1 October 2026
In short: your makeup list is yours. We only keep what's needed to show you your collection, we never sell it, and there are no ads or trackers. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

If you don't sign in

Everything you add (photos, brands, shades, notes) is saved only on your own device, inside your browser. We can't see it. Clearing your browser's website data deletes it, so use "Save backup file" now and then.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. If you do, we save these things online so you can see your collection on all your devices:

  • Your email address, to send you sign-in codes and recognize your account.
  • Your items: category, brand, product, shade, color, notes, barcode and dates.
  • Your item photos.

This data is stored with Supabase, the database service the app uses. Database rules make sure only you can read or change your items and photos. Sign-in emails are sent through our email provider.

We use your data only to run the app. We don't sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI.

Other services the app talks to

  • Barcodes: scanning happens on your device. The barcode number is only compared with your own collection and is never sent to a product database.
  • Code and fonts: parts of the app (the barcode reader and sign-in code) load from jsDelivr, and the font loads from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services can see your device's IP address when they send the files.
  • Hosting: the app's web pages are hosted by Hostinger (and a copy on GitHub Pages).

Cookies and storage

The app saves your collection and, if you sign in, your sign-in session in your browser's storage. That's all. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

  • One item: open it and tap "Remove from collection". It's removed online too.
  • Your whole account: tap the person button on the home screen → "Delete my account". This permanently deletes your account, email address, items and photos from our online storage.
  • Data on your phone: remove items in the app, or clear this website's data in your browser settings.

Questions

Email hello@danitalab.com and we'll help. If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top.
